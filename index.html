The TJR setup in 4 steps
1
Potential: a liquidity sweep
After 9:30, price pokes through a level where stops sit (Asian, London or prior-day high/low), then snaps back. Taking out lows sets up longs; taking out highs sets up shorts.
2
Confirmation: a break of structure
A candle closes beyond the last 5 candles in the opposite direction of the sweep. That says the move is turning.
3
Continuation: enter at the fair value gap
The break leaves a gap between candles. A limit order goes at its midpoint. TJR's entry macro is 9:50–10:10; testing on NQ found more good setups when entries run until 11:00 and sweeps count from the 8:30 open, so the Lab uses that (Lab → Structure study). 9:30–9:50 is still the manipulation window: no entries.
4
Exit: the next pool of liquidity
Target the next high or low on the other side (or 2× the risk). The stop goes past the sweep or a fixed distance. Flat by 12:00, no matter what.

Edge Conditions
When VIX is 15–20
Moderate volatility — ideal for intraday momentum plays. Trends tend to hold. ORB and impulse pullback setups have highest win rate.
Optimal VIX 15-20
When VIX is below 13
Low volatility — range-bound chop. Mean-reversion at extremes. Reduce size, tighten stops. Avoid momentum setups; fade moves to range boundaries instead.
Careful VIX <13
When VIX is above 25
High volatility — wide ranges, fast moves, whipsaws. Reduce size by 50%. Only trade highest-conviction setups. Wider stops required or sit out entirely.
Caution VIX 25+
Gap Up > 0.5% with Premarket Trend
Bullish bias day. Look for VWAP support on first pullback. ORB high breakout is primary setup. Fade gaps only if immediate reversal on open.
Bull Setup Gap Play
Gap Down > 0.5% with Premarket Trend
Bearish bias day. Look for VWAP resistance on first bounce. ORB low breakdown is primary setup. Bounce fades are secondary setup.
Bear Setup Gap Play
FOMC / CPI / Major News Days
No trades before the event. After the initial reaction (first 5 candles), look for post-news momentum trade in the direction of the move if it's clean. Avoid overtrading the chop after the first move.
