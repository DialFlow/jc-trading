Playbook
Opening Range Breakout (ORB)
Wait for the first 5-minute candle to close. Mark high/low. Trade the breakout of that range with volume confirmation. Best when premarket trend aligns with direction.
9:30 Open 5m Chart Momentum
VWAP Reclaim / Rejection
Price returns to VWAP after a gap or move. Long on reclaim from below with volume. Short on failed retest from above. Best in trending sessions, not chop.
Intraday Mean Revert
Impulse Pullback
Strong directional impulse move on above-average volume. Wait for a 3-candle pullback to prior resistance (now support) or VWAP. Enter on the first close in the direction of the impulse.
Trend 2nd Entry
Failed Auction / Trap
Price breaks a key level (premarket high, prior day high/low) and immediately reverses. Trade the trap — fade the breakout when the candle closes back below/above the level.

Is it real? As coded, no. It lost on every trade over 7 weeks.

That doesn't settle TJR itself. 7 trades is too few to judge any strategy, and a free TradingView plan only loads about 7 weeks of 5-minute data.

What the test does show:

  • A "sweep" happened on 37 of 38 days, so a sweep alone filters out almost nothing. The setup needs a stricter definition of which level matters.
  • Stops right behind the sweep wick get hit by normal noise. Most losers were stopped before the move.

Next tests worth running: a wider stop (20–25 pts) or a stop under the second sweep, requiring a displacement candle for the break of structure, and the same rules on NQ with a scaled stop.
